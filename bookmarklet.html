Wiki-Masters Auto

Opens your packs on its own, waits when you run out, and dismisses the “are you still there” popup.

⚡ Wiki-Auto

Drag it to your bookmarks bar. Clicking it on this page does nothing.

Can’t drag? Paste the link as the URL of a new bookmark.

How to use it

  1. Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Open the game and go to your packs.
  3. Click the bookmark. A small widget appears in the bottom-right corner.

The counter shows how many packs it has opened. ⏸ pauses, ■ stops, and clicking the bookmark again also stops it.
